# Bowly AI Demo Script

Target length: **2 minutes 40 seconds**. Keep the entire video under three
minutes.

## 0:00-0:20 — Problem and Track

Introduce Bowly as a Qwen Cloud **EdgeAgent** for children's violin practice.
State the core principle: good coaching must know when to speak and when to stay
quiet.

## 0:20-0:45 — Architecture

Show the architecture diagram. Explain that pose and audio perception run on
the phone or tablet for latency and privacy, while structured metrics go to
Qwen Cloud for coaching and reflection.

## 0:45-1:35 — Live Practice

1. Start a practice session and allow camera and microphone permissions.
2. Show smooth, minimal posture guides.
3. Keep playing and point out that Bowly does not interrupt the phrase.
4. Pause naturally and show one short delayed coaching message.
5. Mention that raw camera images and audio are not uploaded to Qwen.

## 1:35-2:10 — Qwen Cloud

1. Show the `Qwen Live · qwen3.7-plus` status.
2. End the session and open the child-friendly practice recap.
3. Show effort stars, one clear win, one mini challenge, and a newly unlocked
   badge.
4. Open Parent Details and show Qwen-generated reflection plus recent trends.

## 2:10-2:30 — Edge Resilience

Briefly show or describe Mock fallback. Explain that posture tracking, audio
analysis, and session recording continue when connectivity is weak.

## 2:30-2:40 — Close

Close with: "Bowly turns private edge perception into gentle Qwen-powered
reflection, without taking children out of the music."
